Puchong schools, and what tends to get missed here

Puchong has a large number of SJKC families alongside national schools, and that mix produces a specific blind spot we see repeatedly.

  • A child carrying Mandarin, Bahasa Melayu and English at once has an enormous language load. A real reading difficulty hides inside it very easily, because everyone has a ready explanation: he is just weak in Mandarin, she will catch up in BM. Years can pass on that explanation.
  • The test that cuts through it is simple. Count vocabulary and reading progress across all the child's languages together, not one at a time. A genuine difficulty shows up in the total.
  • Character recognition in Mandarin and phonetic decoding in BM and English draw on different skills, so a child can look fine in one and struggle badly in another without that meaning anything is wrong. It is the pattern across all three that tells you something.

Other help around Puchong

  • NASOM has centres in Selangor and is the usual first call for autism-specific questions.
  • The Dyslexia Association of Malaysia offers assessment and intervention in the Klang Valley if reading is the main worry.
  • Your district JKM office can point you to PDK community programmes and explain OKU registration.
The public route, honestly

Getting a formal assessment in Puchong

We are not a clinic and we cannot diagnose. If your child needs a formal assessment, this is the public route from Puchong. It costs very little, and for many families it is the right first step regardless of whether they ever come to us.

Start at your Klinik Kesihatan

Klinik Kesihatan Puchong Batu 14 is the main government clinic for the area. Ask specifically for a developmental or learning assessment referral rather than a general check-up. Take anything the school has written down, plus your own notes from home. Written observations are taken more seriously than a verbal account.

Klinik Kesihatan Puchong Batu 14 · 1 Jalan PU 5, Batu 14, 47100 Puchong · 03-8061 1814 · Mon–Thu 7.30–12.30 and 1.45–4.30, Fri 7.30–12.00 and 2.30–4.30

What to expect

  • Puchong referrals generally go to a government hospital with a paediatric department serving the district. Ask the clinic to write down which one covers you.
  • Ask to join the waiting list at the referral visit rather than at a later appointment. It can save months.
  • The public route costs very little. Private psychoeducational assessment in the Klang Valley typically runs into the low thousands of ringgit.
  • Request a hearing and vision check at the same visit if none has been done recently. Both are quick and rule out a lot.

A long wait is not a reason to wait. Practical support at home and school can begin now, and a term of targeted help gives the specialist something concrete to work from.

Common questions

What Puchong families ask us

How far is the centre from Puchong?

15 to 20 minutes via the LDP. Our centre is at 1st Floor, 10, Jalan USJ 10/1, Taipan Business Centre, Subang Jaya. And after screening, eligible children can be supported online, so the drive never decides whether your child gets help.

What does the screening cost?

RM120 per screening area, report included. We screen the area of concern rather than running a blanket test, so most Puchong families pay for one or two. Anything further is discussed with you before it happens.

Is this a clinical diagnosis?

No, and we say so in the report itself. We identify whether an inherent difficulty appears to be there and where it sits. For a formal diagnosis the public route from Puchong starts at Klinik Kesihatan Puchong Batu 14, and we will point you there when that is the right step.

Can my child be supported online?

Eligible children can, after an in-person screening. Eligibility depends on age, the type of challenge and how your child engages online, and we will advise honestly rather than sell you sessions that will not work.

My child's school raised the concern. What now?

Ask them to put it in writing before you come. A written observation from a Puchong teacher who sees your child every day carries real weight with a specialist later. With your permission we send findings and classroom suggestions back to the school.

Do we need a diagnosis to start?

No diagnosis is required for screening or for any of our programmes. Many families come to us precisely because they want to understand what is happening before pursuing anything formal.
