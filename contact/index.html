Getting here

By car. Taipan Business Centre sits just off Persiaran Kewajipan, reached from the LDP, the NPE or the Federal Highway depending on which side of the Klang Valley you start from. Most families from Petaling Jaya and Puchong are with us inside half an hour outside peak periods.

By LRT. Taipan station is on the Kelana Jaya line and is a short walk away, which makes us reachable without a car from most of Petaling Jaya, and from Puchong by changing at Putra Heights.

On the day. Tell us roughly when you plan to arrive and we will send exact directions and parking guidance on WhatsApp. It saves circling.

What happens next

After you get in touch

1

We reply within one business day

Usually much sooner on WhatsApp. You will speak to someone who works with the children, not a call centre.

2

A free 15-minute conversation

You describe what you are seeing. We tell you honestly whether it sounds like something we can help with, or whether you would be better served elsewhere.

3

A suggested next step

That might be a screening, a programme, or simply a few things to try at home first. There is no obligation and no pressure at any point.

Not ready to talk yet? The free 2-minute check asks a few questions about your child and gives you something concrete to bring to the conversation.

Send us a message

Tell us about your child.

A few lines is plenty. What you have noticed, how old they are, and what worries you most. We will come back with a suggested next step, free and with no obligation.

The Lodestone Centre offers personalised, strengths-first support for children with ADHD, dyscalculia, dyslexia and other learning differences in Subang Jaya, Petaling Jaya and the Klang Valley, including dysgraphia, autism, speech delay and slower learning, as well as gifted children and those who simply learn at their own pace. Every program is built around the individual child. The Lodestone Centre is an inclusive provider of non-curricular teaching, personal development training, motivational courses, educational support workshops and character development workshops. Our screening and programs are designed to support learning and growth, and are not a substitute for formal schooling or clinical diagnosis.
